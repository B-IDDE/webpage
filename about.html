About B‑IDDE

The Berlin Infectious Disease Dynamics & Epidemiology (B‑IDDE) network is a community of researchers, data scientists, and public health professionals based in Berlin. Our mission is to strengthen collaborative research and capacity in infectious disease modelling and advanced analytical methods.

We connect individuals working across disciplines—epidemiology, mathematics, statistics, computer science, public health, and beyond—to share insights, discuss emerging research, and promote best practices in modelling infectious diseases.

B‑IDDE hosts regular seminars, hands‑on training sessions, and informal meet‑ups aimed at supporting both established experts and early career researchers. Through these events, we aim to foster a supportive community, encourage interdisciplinary collaboration, and help advance infectious disease modelling efforts locally and internationally.
