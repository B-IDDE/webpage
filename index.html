About B-IDDE

Aims: To connect those based in Berlin with an interest in infectious disease dynamics and advanced analytical methods. To update each other on recent and current research and other relevant developments in the field To give early career researchers an opportunity to informally share their research, practice their presentation skills and get input from a range of experts.
